06 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before you hand over your phone.

Will they see the app name?

No. The stranger-facing flow shows only a neutral question and response choices. Your dashboard and app branding stay hidden.

What happens if they say no?

They see a calm acknowledgement and the flow ends. Going back cannot expose your private home screen.

Where is their number stored?

Only on your device. ColdHello does not run servers that receive your contacts, preferences, or approach history.

When does a free approach count?

It counts when you tap START, whether the other person accepts or declines. You can start up to 10 free approaches per installation, or save up to 5 contacts, whichever happens first.
